YAIT Mockups: index and main path

YAIT mockups

YAIT is a local tool that works out the capture date of every photo and video in a folder, then renames, sorts and captions them, without ever overwriting a file and with every change undoable. These pages are static drawings of its screens, made before any code, to judge them on paper. They are filled with file names from the project's own test collection.

The striped bar at the top of each page is only there to move around these mockups. It will not exist in the application.

Main path

Follow it from the first step to the last; each page links to the next.

  1. Home: choose the folder to work on. Judge: does a newcomer know what to paste?
  2. Collection summary: check this is the right folder. Judge: is the path unmistakable?
  3. Job progress: the scan, running, stopped, finished. Judge: is “stopped” clear about what was kept?
  4. Inventory: every file, its date, and where the date came from. Judge: are doubtful dates easy to spot?
  5. Media detail: why one file got its date. Judge: would a stranger understand the explanation?
  6. Rename plan: the frozen before/after list. Judge: is the direction of the change obvious?
  7. Collisions: files fighting for one name, and who wins. Judge: is the tie-break order understandable?
  8. Organize: tree or flat, and what happens to undated files. Judge: is the undated difference between modes clear?
  9. History and undo: every operation, undone newest first. Judge: is the order guard understandable, and undo hard to trigger by accident?

Off the main path

  • Metadata editor: review and fix captions and dates. Judge: are divergent rows easy to find?
  • Empty and error states: nine situations that go wrong or are empty. Judge: does each message say what to do next?
