# RG-1.2 — Dates carried by folder names

A photograph inherits the date of the folder someone filed it in. On archives
recovered from CDs this is often the only trustworthy source: the camera clock
may never have been set, but the person who burned the disc knew what the
evening was.

## Rules

| Rule | Status | Decides |
|---|---|---|
| `UC-13-folder-date-formats` | ▶ | Which written forms count as a folder date |
| `UC-14-deepest-folder-wins` | ▶ | Which dated ancestor wins when several carry a date |
| `UC-15-folder-french-month` | ▶ | `22 JUILLET 2002` → month precision |
| `UC-16-batch-suffix-is-not-a-month` | ⊘ | `2006-2` is the second disc of 2006, not February |

## Boundaries

Reading a date from the file's own name is
[RG-1.1](../RG-1.1-filename-dates/group.md). Whether a folder date beats the
camera clock is [RG-1.4](../RG-1.4-arbitration/group.md).
